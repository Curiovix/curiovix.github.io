Designed for music lovers.
Built with respect for privacy.

On-Device Taste Personalization

Melodiq learns your music taste, favorite genres, and frequent tracks entirely on your device using sandboxed local Hive storage (allowBackup="false"). Zero personal listening data is uploaded to cloud profiles.

Regional Music Discovery

Explore curated regional music trends and playlists effortlessly. Features automated language defaults on en_IN devices backed by periodic, encrypted HTTPS region lookups (ipwho.is with 2-hour TTL cache).

Real-Time Synced Lyrics

Follow along word-for-word with rich, synchronized lyrics display retrieved via public lyrics requests (LrcLib), keeping you immersed in the emotional flow of every song.

Sleep Timer with Volume Fade

Set a gentle bedtime countdown to smoothly fade out volume and pause audio automatically when you fall asleep. Conserves battery life and prevents unexpected background data usage.

Offline Local Audio Playback

Seamlessly scan and enjoy your device's local audio files with full offline playback support via READ_MEDIA_AUDIO. Your private audio files stay strictly on your Android device.

Hands-Free Voice Search

Find songs, artists, and albums instantly with optional microphone voice search (RECORD_AUDIO). Uses system speech-to-text; voice audio is never recorded, stored, or uploaded to any server.

Your listening habits
stay on your device.

Melodiq stores your listening history, favorites, custom playlists, and taste profiles locally on your device in sandboxed Hive boxes (allowBackup="false"). Curiovix operates no backend servers collecting user accounts or personal listening databases.

Ad-supported via Google AdMob with Google UMP consent compliance in fail-closed mode. Muted Now-Playing Native ads never disrupt your listening.

Local Hive Storage

Your listening history, favorites, and playlists are saved strictly in your device's local Hive database with allowBackup="false". Never uploaded to cloud profiles.

Zero User Accounts

No registration, no passwords, and no cloud profile. Open the app and start streaming or playing local files immediately.

Offline Media Playback

Plays your on-device audio files without needing an active internet connection. Your local music library stays under your control.

AdMob & UMP Consent

Google AdMob integration with Google UMP consent compliance in fail-closed mode, and strictly muted native ads.
