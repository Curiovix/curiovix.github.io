Designed for music lovers.
Built with respect for privacy.

On-Device Taste Personalization

Melodiq learns your music taste, favorite genres, and frequent tracks entirely on your device using local Hive storage. Zero listening data is uploaded to cloud profiles.

Regional Music Discovery

Explore curated regional music trends and playlists effortlessly. Features automated language defaults on en_IN devices backed by periodic, encrypted HTTPS region lookups.

Real-Time Synced Lyrics

Follow along word-for-word with rich, synchronized lyrics display retrieved via public lyrics requests (LrcLib), keeping you immersed in the emotional flow of every song.

Built-In Sleep Timer

Set a gentle bedtime countdown to pause audio automatically when you fall asleep. Conserve device battery life and prevent unexpected background data use overnight.

Offline Local Audio Playback

Seamlessly scan and enjoy your device's local audio files with full offline playback support. Your private audio files stay strictly on your Android device.

Hands-Free Voice Search

Find songs, artists, and albums instantly with optional microphone voice search. Fast, convenient query processing without saving audio recordings to any server.

Your listening habits
stay on your device.

Melodiq stores your listening history, favorites, custom playlists, and taste profiles locally on your device in lightweight Hive boxes. We never require user accounts, passwords, or personal credentials.

Ad-supported via Google AdMob with Google UMP consent compliance. Offline local playback requires no internet connection.
