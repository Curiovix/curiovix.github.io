Melodiq Privacy Policy

How Melodiq protects your privacy and handles on-device data.

Effective Date: November 2026
About Melodiq (v1.0.0, Developer: Curiovix, Contact: curiovix.support@gmail.com): Modern, lightweight Android music player and audio discovery companion featuring on-device taste personalization, regional music discovery, real-time synced lyrics, sleep timer with smooth volume fade-out, offline local audio playback, and hands-free voice search.

1. Local-First Architecture (No User Accounts)

Melodiq requires no user registration, login, password, or cloud profile. Favorites, custom playlists, listening history, and taste profiles are stored strictly on-device in sandboxed local Hive storage (allowBackup="false"). Curiovix operates no backend servers collecting user accounts or personal listening databases.

2. Advertising & Google UMP Consent

Melodiq integrates Google AdMob (google_mobile_ads) to display App Open ads and muted Now-Playing Native ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction diagnostics for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention. For users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Melodiq uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK in fail-closed mode to obtain consent before loading ads. Users can reset or opt out of personalized advertising anytime via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads or the in-app Manage Ad Consent option.

3. Encrypted Regional Language Lookup (ipwho.is)

When "Auto" language mode is selected on en_IN devices, Melodiq performs a periodic encrypted HTTPS region lookup via ipwho.is (cached locally on-device with a 2-hour TTL) solely to tailor regional music defaults (e.g., Telugu in Andhra Pradesh/Telangana). As part of any standard HTTPS network request, the external regional lookup provider (ipwho.is) receives the device's IP address to resolve approximate region/state; Curiovix does not collect, log, or retain IP addresses on any server. Devices with explicit system locales resolve language 100% locally with zero network requests.

4. Public Lyrics & Search Metadata Requests (LrcLib & Stream Discovery)

Individual search queries, track metadata, and synced lyrics requests (LrcLib) are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS (usesCleartextTraffic="false") to fetch requested audio streams, cover artwork, and lyrics. These individual requests are ephemeral and are not tied to any user identity or account.

5. Device Permissions (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO & Optional RECORD_AUDIO)

Storage/media audio permission (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO on Android 13+ / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and below) is used solely to scan and play audio files already stored locally on the user's device. Optional microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) is requested only when the user taps the voice search button and uses Android's system speech-recognition service (which may process voice audio via Google or device speech services depending on the user's Android speech settings) to convert speech into a search query; Curiovix never records, stores, or uploads voice audio.

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Because all user preferences and history reside locally on-device, users can permanently delete all stored app data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Melodiq → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app. Contact curiovix.support@gmail.com for any privacy inquiries.

7. Third-Party Services & Privacy Policies

External services integrated into or accessed by Melodiq operate under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob & Google UMP — Google Privacy Policy
  • LrcLib (Public Lyrics Provider) — LrcLib Website
  • ipwho.is (Regional Lookup API) — ipwho.is Privacy
