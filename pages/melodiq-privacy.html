Melodiq Privacy Policy

How Melodiq protects your privacy and handles on-device data.

Last updated: November 2026
Summary: Melodiq is built from the ground up to respect your privacy. All your listening preferences, taste profiles, history, playlists, and favorites are stored 100% locally on your Android device using Hive. We do not require accounts, logins, or passwords, and we never upload your personal audio files or listening data to our servers.

1. Who We Are

Melodiq is developed and distributed by Curiovix ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to transparency and describes how the Melodiq application ("App") handles information when you use it on your Android device.

Official Contact: curiovix.support@gmail.com

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No User Accounts: We do not require you to register, log in, or provide usernames, passwords, phone numbers, or email addresses.
  • No Personal Identity Profiles: We do not collect or track personal identities or associate listening activity with individuals.
  • No Server-Side Audio or Listening Uploads: Your listening history, playlists, favorites, and local audio files are never uploaded to, hosted on, or processed by Curiovix servers.
  • No Sale of Data: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user data to third parties.

3. 100% Local On-Device Hive Storage

Melodiq utilizes lightweight, on-device Hive storage boxes exclusively located in your device's private app sandbox. This includes:

  • Taste Personalization: The App analyzes your listening patterns, preferred genres, and most-played tracks locally to power on-device recommendations and personalized feeds. This algorithm operates entirely on your device.
  • Favorites & Playlists: Tracks you favorite or organize into playlists are saved strictly in your local Hive storage.
  • Playback Preferences: Settings such as sleep timer durations, audio quality selections, and theme preferences remain strictly on-device.

Because this data resides solely on your Android device, Curiovix has zero access to your taste profile or listening records.

4. Advertising & Google AdMob Compliance

Melodiq is a free application supported by advertising through Google AdMob (using the official google_mobile_ads SDK).

  • Google UMP Consent: Melodiq incorporates the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to comply with international privacy standards (including the European General Data Protection Regulation — GDPR — and UK data protection frameworks). Where applicable, users receive standard consent dialogs to review and configure their advertising preferences.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Third-party ad networks (Google and its certified partners) may process standard mobile advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID) and non-identifiable technical diagnostics to deliver contextual advertisements.
  • Muted Native Ads: Any native advertisements displayed on the Now-Playing screen are strictly muted by default to preserve an uninterrupted listening environment.

For detailed information on how Google processes ad data, please review the Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Technologies.

5. Regional Language Discovery (ipwho.is)

To deliver relevant regional music discovery and configure sensible regional language defaults for users on Indian English (en_IN) devices, Melodiq makes a periodic, encrypted HTTPS query to the IP lookup service ipwho.is.

  • Local 2-Hour TTL Caching: The regional lookup response is stored strictly in your local device cache with a 2-hour time-to-live (TTL). The App will not make additional lookup requests while the cache remains valid.
  • No Personal Information: No names, account data, or user profiles are sent with this lookup. Curiovix does not log, track, or retain user IP addresses on any server.

6. Public Synced Lyrics & Metadata Requests (LrcLib)

When playing tracks, Melodiq can retrieve synchronized and plain-text lyrics from public lyric repositories, specifically LrcLib, via encrypted HTTPS.

  • These requests transmit only public metadata — namely the track title and artist name — necessary to look up matching lyrics.
  • No user identifiers, device credentials, or listening histories are attached to lyric queries.

7. Device Permissions Disclosed

Melodiq requests only permissions strictly necessary to provide media playback and user-initiated features:

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Allows the App to scan, index, and play audio files stored on your local device storage. Your files remain on your phone; they are never uploaded to any server.
  • RECORD_AUDIO (Optional Microphone Access): Used exclusively when you tap the voice search button to search for songs hands-free. Audio is processed into text via standard Android speech recognition; voice recordings are never saved, recorded in the background, or transmitted to Curiovix.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to stream online audio streams, fetch public lyrics, check cached regional discovery defaults, and load AdMob advertisements.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required by Android to keep audio playing smoothly when the app is in the background or the screen is locked, and to display notification playback controls.

8. User Data Deletion & Storage Controls

Because all personal preferences and playlists are stored locally on your device in Hive, you maintain complete sovereignty over your data at all times:

  • Instant Data Deletion: You can completely and irreversibly erase all local Hive databases, history, cached lyrics, and settings at any time by opening your phone's Settings → Apps → Melodiq → Storage → Tap “Clear Data” and “Clear Cache”.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling Melodiq permanently deletes all application storage, local databases, and temporary cache files from your device.
  • Assistance: For questions or data management guidance, you can always contact us at curiovix.support@gmail.com.

9. Children’s Privacy

Melodiq is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children.
