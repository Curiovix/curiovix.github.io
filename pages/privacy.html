Privacy Policy

How Curiovix and StorySplitz handle your information.

Last updated: August 2026
Summary: StorySplitz processes all video entirely on your device. We do not receive or store your video content on Curiovix servers. We collect anonymised crash/analytics data via Firebase, and advertising is served by third-party providers.

1. Who We Are

StorySplitz is developed and distributed by Curiovix. This Privacy Policy explains how the StorySplitz application ("App") collects, uses, and handles information when you use it.

Contact: curiovix.support@gmail.com

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not upload, receive, or store your video files on Curiovix servers.
  • We do not collect your name, email, or personal identity information.
  • We do not require account registration or login.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. On-Device Video Processing

All video processing in StorySplitz - story splitting, compression, GIF creation, watermark tools, audio extraction, and format conversion - is performed entirely on your Android device using the FFmpeg engine bundled within the App. Your video content does not leave your device for processing by Curiovix. Output files are saved to your local device storage and remain under your control.

4. Firebase Analytics

StorySplitz uses Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) to collect anonymised usage information. This may include which features are used, session duration, device type, OS version, and general geographic region. This data is aggregated and anonymised. Google Privacy Policy.

5. Firebase Crashlytics

StorySplitz uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) for automatic crash reports including stack traces, device model, OS version at crash time. Crash reports do not include video content or personal identity information. Google Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising

StorySplitz is free and supported by advertising via StartApp (Start.io). The App shows optional rewarded ads (earn credits) and brief interstitial ads after successful exports. Ad providers may collect device identifiers for contextual advertising. Start.io Privacy Policy.

7. Device Permissions

  • Storage / Media access: To read video files and save output files.
  • Internet: Required for advertising and Firebase reporting.
  • Foreground Service: To continue processing while you use other apps.
  • Notification: To display processing progress.

We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

8. Data Retention

Curiovix does not retain your video content. In-app data (credit balance, history) is stored locally on your device and removed when you uninstall the App.

9. Children's Privacy

StorySplitz is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Analytics - Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics - Google Privacy Policy
  • Start.io - Start.io Privacy Policy

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised date.
